## How they're made

- **Footage:** the real store, captured on a phone-sized screen, plus the collection's product photography. Every screen in the reels is the working store, not a mock-up.
- **Music:** original. Each track is a dark trap beat at 140 BPM in F minor, synthesised from scratch for this project (no samples or licensed audio). All four share one sound palette, so the brand sounds the same everywhere. Mastered to −14 LUFS, the level Instagram and Facebook normalise to.
- **Edit:** every cut, text pop and tap lands on the beat, because frames are rendered against the music's bar grid (12 bars per reel).
- **Built for sound-off:** each line of the message is on screen in large type, and the important content stays clear of the areas Instagram covers with its own buttons.

## Posting them

1. Upload the file as a Reel. The original audio is safe to keep. To ride a trend instead, mute it and pick a sound from Instagram's library, which is licensed for the platform.
2. Cover image: choose the frame with the phone or the product, not the title card.
3. Put the matching link in the caption or link sticker, with UTM tags from the [campaign plan](launch-campaign.md#tracking-links), for example `dapper-denn.netlify.app/drop?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=anime-legends-drop`.
